\section{Empirical Evaluation and Benchmark Results}
\label{sec:evaluation}

This section presents the experimental validation of our decentralized, autonomic DDoS mitigation framework within an Istio service mesh deployed on Kubernetes. We evaluate the framework across five complementary operational scenarios, comparing its detection latency, mitigation overhead, blast radius containment, machine learning inference characteristics, and closed-loop two-stage autonomous recovery against both centralized coordinator baselines and unconstrained global models.
